How it works

You keep your number. Nothing else changes.

We set it up for you

You tell us your services, your area and what counts as an emergency. We train it on that, under your business name.

Missed calls go to it

Your phone rings as normal. If you don't answer, the call forwards to the receptionist. You stay in control.

You get the details

After each call you get the customer's name, number, address and problem, so you know who to ring back first.

What it does

A careful message-taker, not a salesperson.

It will

  • Answer day or night, in your business's name
  • Ask what's wrong and how urgent it is
  • Take the name, number and address
  • Give basic safety advice you approve, like turning off the stopcock

It won't

  • Quote prices or promise a time
  • Pretend to be a person. If asked, it says it's an AI
  • Book anything without you agreeing to it first
  • Replace you. It just stops the call going to voicemail
