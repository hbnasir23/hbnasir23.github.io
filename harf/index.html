FAQ

Questions

How long does delivery take?

We print each hoodie after you confirm it, so it ships in 5 to 7 days. Delivery inside Pakistan usually takes another 2 to 4 days.

Do I pay first?

No. You pay cash when the hoodie arrives. We only ask you to confirm your order on WhatsApp first.

Can I write any word?

Almost. Type it in English letters or Urdu and we'll write it properly in Urdu and show you before printing. We don't print brand names, abuse, or political or religious slogans.

What if it's printed wrong?

If the print doesn't match what we confirmed with you, we'll reprint it free.

Are the photos real?

They're previews made from real hoodie photos. Your hoodie is printed to order, so the print placement can vary slightly.
